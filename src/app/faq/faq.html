Questions

Before you start

Is this an AI or real people?

Both. Your Double handles the everyday — tracking, reminders, explaining, drafting. When a question needs a licence behind it, it hands you a verified human and stays in the thread.

Where do its answers come from?

Your own records. Every answer is grounded in the documents and accounts you connected, with a citation showing the source. It doesn't guess on things it can't back up.

Can advisors see my data?

Only the thread you personally open with them, and only what you send in it. Your wider profile stays private and is never forwarded in the background.

How long does setup take?

About fifteen minutes to a useful Double. Connect a couple of sources, answer a few questions, and it's ready — then it keeps improving as you use it.

What if I delete my account?

Export everything first as readable files. After deletion we remove your data within 30 days and confirm in writing.
